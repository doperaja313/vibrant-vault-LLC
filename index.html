About Vibrant Vault

A purchasing partner built for the long term

We are a U.S.-based e-commerce and product distribution company specializing in wholesale sourcing, online and retail arbitrage, private label, generic products, and e-commerce distribution. We work with brands, manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, and other authorized supply partners to source quality products for customers across the United States.

Our approach is straightforward: reliable communication, consistent purchasing, and a focus on product quality. We operate across several e-commerce channels and product categories, which lets us build steady, repeatable volume with the supply partners we work with.
